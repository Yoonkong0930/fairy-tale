```prompt
Create a FACE PARTS SHEET for rigging the character "Momo" (the baby rabbit in the reference images). Draw the parts exactly in the same style, line weight and colors as the reference.

Background: perfectly flat solid pure green (#00FF00) chroma-key background. No shadows, no gradients.

Layout: a neat grid of 4 columns x 4 rows. Each cell contains exactly ONE isolated part, centered, at the scale it appears on her face, with plenty of green space around it. Parts never touch. No face, no fur behind the parts. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT OR LABELS anywhere.

Row 1 — single EYES (one eye per cell):
 1. open eye: large round glossy dark-brown eye with a soft lighter-brown crescent at the bottom, and exactly two white highlights: one large round at the upper-left and one small round at the lower-right.
 2. happy closed eye: upward curved arc line (^).
 3. blink closed eye: gentle downward curved line.
 4. surprised eye: white round eye with a thin dark outline and a small dark-brown pupil.
Row 2:
 1. neutral eyebrow: thin short gentle arc.
 2. worried eyebrow: thin line tilted up toward the center.
 3. sad eyebrow: thin line tilted up toward the center, slightly curved.
 4. NOSE only: the small soft pink triangle nose.
Row 3 — MOUTHS only (NO nose):
 1. neutral small "ω" mouth.
 2. happy open smile with pink tongue.
 3. talking small: slightly open small mouth.
 4. talking medium: half-open rounded mouth with a little pink tongue.
Row 4 — MOUTHS only (NO nose):
 1. talking big: wide open round mouth with pink tongue.
 2. sad: small downturned curve.
 3. worried: small wobbly wavy mouth.
 4. surprised: small round "o" mouth.
```
